Old School Days

Life in senior high school was very hard, especially when you have a shy personality.
Being “mahiyain” made me wonder what would happen during those years. I didn't know if I would be able
to adapt to everything, make new friends, or just stay quiet and do nothing. People always said that senior
high school was very different from high school, and honestly, I was scared that I wouldn't fit in

But then, I found my friends that will really make sure that no one was ever left out.
Even when things were difficult, having them by my side made everything easier and more fun.
Because of them, I was able to enjoy my high school life and create memories that I will always cherish.
After school, all we wanted to do was hang out together. We didn't need expensive places or big plans.
As long as we were together, we were already having fun.
